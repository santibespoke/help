# Custom Styles

We already offer dozens of shoe styles for Made-to-Order production as well as BULK Ready-to-Wear collections. Also, you can order small batches of unique and seasonal styles using your RTW Platform.&#x20;

However, in addition to those styles, we will be glad to **develop and produce your own shoe styles** (shoe collections) based on your own specifications: your own shoe lasts, shoe patterns, personalized hardware and sole units, etc. New styles developed will remain exclusive for your brand.
