# Enable & Disable Styles

By default, we offer dozens of styles in our MTO platform, however you have the ability to enable / disable styles based on your selling preferences.

To enable / disable a particular MTO style, log into your [Admin Backoffice](https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com/), and select:

```
Configuration > Styles & Prices Config
```

For each particular style + finishing (standard, patina and burnishing) there is a checkbox to enable or disable the style, as well as a price input, where you need to enter the retail price (your selling price to end customers). Also, you will see the production price you will be charged for the manufacturing.&#x20;

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png>)

Once you have made all modifications, click on any of the Update buttons on the page to save the changes. Your designing tool will be automatically updated.

{% hint style="info" %}
Bear in mind that may be different instances of the same style under different categories like standard, patina finishing, burnishing, etc. Remember to fulfill all instances on the [Styles & Prices Config](https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com/backoffice/management.aspx) section.
{% endhint %}
