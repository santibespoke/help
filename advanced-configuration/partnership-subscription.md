# Account & Subscription

By becoming a partner you are granted full access to our comprehensive MTO & BULK design, production, and logistic services using your own brand, as well as to our large array of additional services and products.

On the other hand, you are provided with your own private labeled tools and designing platforms to sell both online and offline. Tech maintenance, cloud hosting, and service support is also included.

## Subscription Plans

Every new account is created as a FREE ACCOUNT by default. It's a fully working demo platform, where you can access all our premium services and products. Everything running under your own brand.&#x20;

See the chart below that explains the differences between a FREE ACCOUNT and a PARTNER ACCOUNT

|                                                                 **FREE ACCOUNT**                                                                 |                             **PARTNER ACCOUNT**                             |
| :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
|                                                           Free, no monthly subscription                                                          |                           Cost is 95 EUR per month                          |
|                                                              No credit card required                                                             |                   Credit card is required for subscription                  |
|                                                                Cancel at any time                                                                |                              Cancel at any time                             |
|                                                          Full access to all our services                                                         |                       Full access to all our services                       |
|                    <p>You are allowed to produce ONLY a first batch of MTO </p><p>orders for sampling and review purposes</p>                    | You are allowed to produce private labeled MTO and BULK orders indefinitely |
| <p>After your first MTO samples are received, </p><p>a paid subscription is required to continue producing MTO orders (single units) with us</p> |                                                                             |
|                          <p>You are allowed to continue placing BULK </p><p>orders indefinitely using a FREE ACCOUNT</p>                         |                                                                             |



The FREE ACCOUNT does not require a monthly payment. It allows you to order your first MTO samples, produced under your brand, and receive them to review our craftsmanship, products, and services for the first time.

It's an easy way to get started and get to know the potential of our private label MTO production program.

After your first MTO samples are received, a paid subscription (PARTNER ACCOUNT) is required to continue producing MTO orders (single units) with us.&#x20;

If you are not interested in MTO production (one by one), and would like to produce BULK orders only (batches of 10+ units per style), then you might want to stick with a FREE ACCOUNT. But bear in mind that you will not be allowed to produce individual orders.

## Your Role as a Partner

We strive to keep our service rates and production margins **as low as possible**.   This is at the very heart of **our core values** and our main goal is passing our partners all benefits and price reductions we can accomplish, either by achieving large production volumes at the factories, improving our internal strategies and logistics, or negotiating discounts with suppliers.

As a production office, we chose the SaaS (Software-as-a-Service) model to fund ourselves. Thanks to our partners monthly membership (95 EUR ), we are able to keep running and improve our production infrastructure and services.

Please, read our full article [Your Role as a Partner](https://mto.bespokefactory.com/your-role-as-a-partner/) for a more in-depth understanding of our long-term vision on the industry and price philosophy.

## Manage Your Subscription

Your partnership subscription can be managed through your [admin account](../admin-backoffice/designing-tools-and-galleries/#the-backoffice):

```
Configuration > Subscription
```

To add a new payment method, or manage your existing ones:

```
Configuration > Account Settings > Payment Methods
```

The partnership subscription will be automatically renewed each month according to billing cycle.

## Cancel Your Subscription

Partnership subscription can be cancelled anytime through your panel. Your account will remain fully operational until the current billing period is over.

```
Configuration > Subscription
```
