# Canada CETA Agreement

## What Is the CETA Agreement?

The Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) is a **free-trade agreement between Canada and the European Union**, which virtually covers all sectors and aspects of Canada-EU trade. For full details we encourage you to visit the [Canada Border Services Agency website](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/publications/cn-ad/cn17-30-eng.html).

#### &#x20;Duty Free Deliveries to Canada

The most remarkable benefit of the CETA is the elimination of tariffs. Prior to its implementation, more than 9,000 tariff lines were used to classify imported goods and apply a tariff. The implementation of CETA allows most European-exported goods into Canada duty free.
