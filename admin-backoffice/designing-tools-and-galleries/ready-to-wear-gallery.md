# Ready-to-Wear Gallery

## The RTW Gallery

To extend our base styles offer for BULK production, we compiled a repository of seasonal shoe styles, with unique sole units, shoe lasts, materials, etc. RTW styles are not available on the 3D Designing Tool nor they can be produced as MTO (single orders)‌. They are exclusively featured for BULK production.

![](https://i.gyazo.com/bda705d28c8845c5f45e18c8ced88e6f.gif)

The RTW platform includes hundreds of styles with lifestyle & studio pictures, ordering price (volume based) and MOQ info. You will be able to order small batches of RTW items (private labeled) using this platform. Shoe styles available on the RTW platform may only be produced in small batches (MOQ => +10 pairs).

## Prices & MOQ

Bulk prices for RTW shoe styles are based on order volume. That is, for each shoe style, you will be presented with **different price tiers depending on ordering quantities**. Click on a shoe style to access its detailed view, including full description, studio images, prices and minimum order quantity. &#x20;

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Captura de pantalla 2019-04-01 a las 15.05.26.jpg>)

**MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) is usually 10 pairs for most styles**, however, there are a few styles that may only be produced in larger batches, for example, +50 pairs. The MOQ for each particular shoe style is specified in its detailed view

## How to Order?

To access the RTW platform, please, **log into your Admin Backoffice and click the “Ready-To-Wear” link**  (left menu). You will need to log in with your admin/agent credentials before being able to see the prices and place an order. The checkout process is exactly the same as a standard bulk order.

There are **filters available on the upper menu bar** that will help you **narrow down your search**. Results will appear as a grid of dynamic images that match your criteria. **Click on a particular shoe style to access a full detailed view.**
