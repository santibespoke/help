# Bazaar Store

As a partner, you have access to a full array of additional services and accessories that complements our MTO production program. Some of these products and services might be ordered through our Bazaar Store, which is accessible through your Admin Backoffice.

![](https://i.gyazo.com/3a4762ccde6d30e9b71ab53b9658295c.gif)

Here, you will find a variety of Accessories, Material Swatches, Stamps Kit, Try-on Sets, etc

Orders are automatically launched to production after payment. Bazaar orders can be shipped individually or be grouped with any other MTO / BULK orders, to save on shipping costs.
