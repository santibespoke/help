The Backoffice
--------------

Through the Admin Backoffice you will be able to [submit new orders](../submit-a-new-order/#place-an-order) and [manage your existing ones](../manage-your-orders.md), as well as submit payments, manage invoices, track live status of your orders, etc.&#x20;

{% endcontent-ref %}

Access the [Admin Backoffice](./#the-backoffice) located at [https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com](https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com) using your admin credentials.

## 3D Designing Tool

![3D Designing Tool in action](https://i.gyazo.com/35fb2d8a7411bb642a90da36d50aea4d.gif)

The 3D Designing Tool works in any web browser, and is fully personalized for your brand. It allows you to [customize ](../submit-a-new-order/#place-an-order)any of the base styles and [submit your MTO and BULK orders to production](../submit-a-new-order/).

{% hint style="info" %}
**Hint:** Using your 3D Tool you can design and produce single orders (MTO) as well as small batches (ready-to-wear collections) with a MOQ of just 10 units per style. Please, take a look at our [Pricing & Production Methods](../../production/pricing-and-production-methods/) article.
{% endhint %}

Manufacturing prices for MTO (single order) production as well as wholesaler costs for BULK production (small batches of 10 items per style) are shown on the top right corner of your Admin 3D Design Tool.

![Both MTO and BULK prices are shown on your Admin 3D Tool](https://i.gyazo.com/ba2875a424270b528ba7384008e48674.gif)

You can access your Admin 3D Tool through your [Admin Backoffice](https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com/). Once you are logged into your [Admin Backoffice](https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com/) click on the top link to access your **Admin 3D Designing Tool**.&#x20;

```
Designing Tools > 3D Designing Tool
```

Learn more about all the features and customizations that can be done using your 3D Designing Tool:

{% content-ref url="3d-designing-tool.md" %}
[3d-designing-tool.md](3d-designing-tool.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Get Inspired Gallery

![Get Inspired gallery, including hundreds of designs](https://i.gyazo.com/43083636c0b8d9c23eda16eeec634ba7.gif)

The Get-Inspired Gallery is a beautiful compilation of thousands of shoe designs available for ordering. Aimed for inspirational purposes, each design features lifestyle and studio pictures, composition details, and a brief description of the style.&#x20;

Access your own private label Get-Inspired gallery through your [Admin Backoffice](https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com/) (Designing Tools menu on the left).

```
Designing Tools > Get Inspired
```

{% hint style="info" %}
Get Inspired items are connected to your 3D designing tool, so you (an your customers) can order the item as it is, or alternatively, load the style on your 3D designing tool for further customization.
{% endhint %}

Learn more about the Get Inspired Gallery, Ordering Methods, Retail Prices and more:

{% content-ref url="get-inspired-gallery.md" %}
[get-inspired-gallery.md](get-inspired-gallery.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Ready-to-Wear Gallery

If you are interested in producing your own Ready-to-Wear collections (that is, the production of small batches with a MOQ of at least 10+ items per style) you should definitely take a look at our RTW styles gallery.

To extend our base styles offer for BULK production, we compiled a repository of seasonal shoe styles, with unique sole units, shoe lasts, materials, etc, which are not available on the 3D Designing Tool for MTO order (one by one).

![Ready-To-Wear Gallery, including dozens of unique styles for bulk production](https://i.gyazo.com/bda705d28c8845c5f45e18c8ced88e6f.gif)

The RTW platform includes hundreds of styles with lifestyle & studio pictures, ordering price (volume based) and MOQ info. You will be able to order small batches of RTW items (private labeled) using this platform.&#x20;

Learn more about the RTW styles available for order on:

[ready-to-wear-gallery.md](ready-to-wear-gallery.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Bazaar Store

As a partner, you have access to a full array of additional services and accessories that complements our MTO production program. Some of these products and services might be ordered through our [Bazaar Store](https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com/backoffice/catalog.aspx), which is accessible through your [Admin Backoffice](https://backoffice.bespokefactory.com/).

![](https://i.gyazo.com/3a4762ccde6d30e9b71ab53b9658295c.gif)

Orders are automatically launched to production after payment. Bazaar orders can be shipped individually or be grouped with any other MTO / BULK orders, to save on shipping costs.
